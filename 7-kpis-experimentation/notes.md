- Experiments can move metrics up short-term, but down long-term.

- Fiddle with numerator or the denominator.

- Novelty effect of experiments: if you change something, it will have a short-term effect, but long-term it will revert to the mean.

- Increased user retention at extreme cost.

> [!NOTE]
> How to select the duration of an experiment to avoid these errors?
> Who is elegible to participate in the experiment?
> What percentage of users should be included in the experiment?

- Leading vs Lagging metrics.
  - Leading metric: "Hours spent studying"
  - Lagging metric: "Number of job offers received"

- Funnel analysis:
  - How many users are in each step of the funnel?
  - How many users drop off at each step?
  - How can we improve the conversion rate at each step?
  - This is where experimentation A/B testing comes in, comparing the conversion rate of two different versions of a product from one step of the funnel to the next.

- How to think like a product manager:
  - Through every step of the funnel ask:
    - What is causing pain?
    - What is sparking joy?
